How to start a claim

  1. 1

    Tell us

    Call, WhatsApp or fill the form below.

  2. 2

    Share documents

    Send us your policy and the papers for your claim.

  3. 3

    Claim is sent

    The claim goes to your insurance company.

  4. 4

    Insurer decides

    The insurance company checks the claim and gives its decision.

Keep these ready

Health claim

  • Policy number and the patient's name
  • Hospital name and city
  • Doctor's reports and bills
  • Date of admission

Car or bike claim

  • Policy number and vehicle number
  • Where and when it happened
  • Photos of the damage
  • Driving licence and RC

If your claim is rejected

  1. 1

    Ask for the reason

    Ask the insurance company to give the reason in writing.

  2. 2

    Complain to the insurer

    Every insurance company has a grievance team.

  3. 3

    Go to the Ombudsman

    If you are not happy with the reply, you can go to the Insurance Ombudsman. It is free.

  4. 4

    Ask us

    Call us if you need help with these steps.
