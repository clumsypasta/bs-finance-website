Before you call

What to have to hand

Nothing here is mandatory to start a claim. It just makes the first call shorter.

Health claims
Policy number and the insured person's name
Hospital name and city
The doctor's diagnosis, in whatever words you have
Date of admission, or the planned date
Whether the condition was declared when you bought the policy
Motor claims
Policy number and vehicle registration number
Where and when it happened
Photographs of the damage, if it is safe to take them
Whether anyone was injured, and whether police were involved
Driving licence of whoever was driving
If a claim is refused

A refusal is not the end of it

Insurers have a formal grievance process, and above that sits the Insurance Ombudsman. Both are free to use. We handle the paperwork for both on your behalf.

01
We ask for the reason in writing

An insurer has to state its grounds. Often the stated ground is something we can answer with one more document.

02
We file with the insurer's grievance officer

Every insurer is required to have one, and to respond within the timeline set by the regulator.

03
We escalate to the Ombudsman

If the grievance route does not resolve it, the Ombudsman hears the case at no cost to you. We prepare and submit the file.
