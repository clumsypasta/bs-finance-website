Title: Car insurance: third-party or comprehensive?
Date: 30-09-2026
Summary: Third-party cover is a must by law. Comprehensive cover also pays for damage to your own car. Here is the difference.
Photo:
-----
## Third-party insurance

- By law (Motor Vehicles Act, 1988), every car and bike on the road in India must have at least third-party insurance.
- It pays for injury or damage that you cause to other people or their property.
- It does **not** pay for damage to your own car.

## Comprehensive insurance

Comprehensive insurance has two parts:

- **Third-party cover**, the part the law asks for.
- **Own damage cover**. It pays for damage to your own car from an accident, fire, theft, flood and similar events.

## Which one should you buy?

Third-party insurance has the lowest price. But if your car is damaged or stolen, you pay for it yourself.

Comprehensive insurance costs more, but it also protects your own car. It is a good choice if your car is new or would cost a lot to repair.

## Add-ons

You can add extra cover to a comprehensive policy. Each add-on costs extra, so pick only what you need. Common add-ons:

- **Zero depreciation**: the insurance company does not cut money for the age of the parts it replaces.
- **Engine protection**: pays for engine damage, for example from water on the road.
- **Roadside assistance**: help if your car stops on the road.

## When you renew

- Check your **No Claim Bonus (NCB)**. It is a discount on your own damage premium for each year you did not make a claim.
- Renew before your policy ends. If it ends, the insurance company may inspect your car before it gives you a new policy.

Title: Insurance claim rejected? Steps you can take
Date: 30-09-2026
Summary: Ask for the reason, complain to the insurance company, and if needed go to the Insurance Ombudsman. It is free.
Photo:
-----
## 1. Read the rejection letter

The insurance company must tell you why it rejected your claim. Read the reason carefully. Then check it against your policy papers.

## 2. Complain to the insurance company

Every insurance company has a team to handle complaints. Write to them and say why you think the claim should be paid. Attach:

- Your policy copy
- The rejection letter
- Your bills, reports or photos

Keep a copy of everything you send.

## 3. Register your complaint with Bima Bharosa

Bima Bharosa is the complaint website of IRDAI, the government body that regulates insurance in India. You can register your complaint there: [bimabharosa.irdai.gov.in](https://bimabharosa.irdai.gov.in).

## 4. Go to the Insurance Ombudsman

The Insurance Ombudsman settles complaints between you and your insurance company. It is free, and you do not need a lawyer.

You can go to the Ombudsman if:

- the insurance company rejected your complaint, or
- it did not reply within one month, or
- you are not happy with its reply.

Go within one year of the insurance company's reply. The amount you ask for must not be more than ₹50 lakh. Find your Ombudsman office on [cioins.co.in](https://www.cioins.co.in).

Title: Health top-up plans: what they are and who needs one
Date: 30-09-2026
Summary: A top-up plan adds more health cover on top of your main policy. See how it works with a simple example.
Photo:
-----
## What is a top-up plan?

A top-up plan gives you extra health cover. It pays only after your hospital bill crosses a fixed amount. This fixed amount is called the **deductible**.

A top-up plan usually costs less than buying a bigger health policy with the same cover.

## A simple example

Your top-up plan has a deductible of ₹5 lakh and a cover of ₹10 lakh. Your hospital bill is ₹8 lakh.

- Your main health policy (or you) pays the first ₹5 lakh.
- The top-up plan pays the other ₹3 lakh.

If the bill is less than ₹5 lakh, the top-up plan pays nothing.

## Top-up and super top-up

A **top-up** looks at each hospital bill on its own. Each bill must cross the deductible.

A **super top-up** adds up all your hospital bills in the policy year. Many small bills together can cross the deductible.

Check which type a plan is before you buy.

## Who should think about a top-up?

- People who have health cover from their office and want more cover of their own
- People whose health policy has a small cover amount
- Families who want more cover on a small budget

## Check these before you buy

- The deductible amount
- Waiting periods for illnesses you already have
- Limits on hospital room rent
- The list of network hospitals near you
