Step 1 and 2 of the rebuild

Study, then direction

What the current site does, what must not break, the brand as it stands, three ways to take it forward, and the one I built.

What I studied

I captured the live page at 1440 and 390 px wide and read all of its markup, CSS and both scripts. Only the home page was supplied. It links to 24 other pages that are not in the folder, so those are listed here but not rebuilt.

Does well: keep

  • The tear. A hoarding you can rip, with real paper physics, a keyboard button, and a still torn composition for reduced motion and no JavaScript. It is the idea people will remember. Kept exactly.
  • Five flat inks with measured contrast, and one brush face that covers Latin and Devanagari.
  • Honesty. No borrowed logos, no invented numbers; the demos are labelled as illustrations.
  • The plumbing. Skip link, live regions, native popover menu, a form that still works without JavaScript, slow-motion and sound switches.

Holds it back: change

  • Twelve full-bleed colour blocks, all at the same volume, each arriving with the same wipe.
  • One list of services shown three ways: three pillars, an eight-tile wall, then a six-item bill.
  • A stat row where two of the four numbers are not facts (8+ beside a button that says 14; 1 partner).
  • Work in progress on show: TODO stickers, empty price boxes, a placeholder portrait.
  • Length: 15,716 px tall on a desktop and 18,785 px on a phone.

The brand as it stands

No brand kit was supplied, so this is read from the site itself.

  1. Keep the wordmark exactly. It is the word Elvana set in Yatra One, yellow on ink. The source says an official logo file is still to come; nothing was redrawn.
  2. Keep the five inks at their current values: yellow #FFD000, red #C8201A, ink #1A1814, blue #1F3FB4, paper #F2F3EF.
  3. Keep the type: Yatra One for statements in English and Hindi, Mukta for everything you read.
  4. Evolve the hoarding from a trick in the hero into the way the whole page is put together.
  5. Replace the wall-to-wall colour, the stat row, the stand-in portrait and initials, and every visible TODO.

Why One Wall

It takes the one thing people already remember about Elvana, the tear, and makes it how the whole page is built instead of a trick at the top. It also answers the complaint about density in the structure itself: one idea per sheet, and the seams do the decorating so the copy does not have to.

Fresh Paste would have been safer and forgettable. Whitewash is the most striking of the three, but it hides the brand's colours from anyone who does not interact, and it puts a Hindi line in the headline that the source marks as still waiting for client approval. It is worth a second look once that line is signed off.
