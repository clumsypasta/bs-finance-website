Copy changes to approve

Every edit is a cut or a move. Nothing was added that the site did not already say, and no number, client or quote was invented. Headings, the page title, the meta description and the structured data are unchanged, apart from the Work heading noted below.

SectionWhat changed in the words
Who we areThe stat row said 8+ service lines and 1 partner for media + tech. It now says 14 services, one partner for media + tech. Fourteen is the site's own count (eight core, six beyond digital).
Who we areVision loses where every idea creates measurable impact. Mission loses for every customer and delivered with speed, transparency and care.
ServicesThe three pillar lines lose their closing clauses (that people stop for, that reach the right audience at the right time, that turn attention into revenue). The fourteen one-liners are shorter and appear on hover; on a phone only the names show.
Our edgeThe lede loses Built inside a technology-first group. The chat demo is four lines. Captions no longer say for a sample institute or for a sample clinic.
How we workStep notes lose Your and and.
Who we serveThe yellow note is now the lede, with give us replaced by a colon.
WorkThe heading changed from First case studies are being written. to This wall is still blank. The first sentence moved into the note under it.
Engagement modelsFrom ₹__ a month, Per campaign ₹__ and Per project ₹__ became a Billed column: By the month, Per campaign, Per project.
PeopleThe founder line loses for customers. Two TODO: focus area stickers and the Team photos to come note are hidden; their data-todo hooks remain in the markup.
FooterThe tagline loses Every campaign measured by what it moves.

The last pass

After the build I captured every section again at 360, 390, 768, 1024, 1440 and 1920 px and went through it as a creative director would.

What people will remember

  • Tearing the hoarding, as before.
  • Tearing their own sector off a flyer and finding it on the enquiry form.
  • A studio confident enough to say, at the biggest size on the page, that its wall is still blank.

The five weakest moments, fixed

What was wrongWhat changed
The founder's name ran into his biography at desktop width.The name is sized to its column and the biography sits clear of it.
The sector tabs' vertical text spilled out of the fringe.Tabs have a fixed height and the names wrap inside them, at every width.
The station boards' posts ended at different heights.The boards share one grid, so every post lands on the same platform line.
On a tablet the red plate covered the note in the blank-wall section, and the Coming soon strip sat on the headline.The plate drops below the frame under 1100 px and the strip is pasted across the frame's top edge.
At 360 px a co-founder's surname broke mid-word and the footer line pushed the page sideways.The team becomes a credits list on phones, and the footer line scales with the screen.
