Three fluid layout values sit beside the steps: --gutter (page edge), --col-gap (between columns) and --section-y (above and below a sheet).

Shape

Paper has square corners: --r-0 for sheets, tags and fields. --r-cut (3px) is only for die-cut tabs. --r-dot is only for the stops on the route line. Rules come in four weights: --bw-hair 2, --bw-rule 3, --bw-bar 6, --bw-slab 10.

The torn edge

Where a sheet ends, it is torn, and older posters show under it. Six edge shapes are generated at build time; each layer is the paper over its own white fibre.

.rip with three layers

Motion

Four springs and three curves, all named. Nothing on the site fades up as you scroll. Movement belongs to the visitor: tearing, pressing, hovering. With reduced motion on, every one of these is instant.

--spring-press
critically damped; a press, over --t-press
--spring-settle
2% overshoot; things landing, over --t-paste
--spring-lift
12% overshoot; a corner lifting, over --t-spring
--spring-wobble
a tab swinging on its last fibre, over --t-wobble
--ease-paste
a squeegee settling, over --t-paste
--ease-tug
gravity; a sheet letting go, over --t-drop
